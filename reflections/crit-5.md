# Crit 5 — 空中花园引水渠 (Hanging Gardens Aqueduct)

## What was the breakthrough that moved the work forward?

The thing that actually moved this forward was that I wanted to build *this*
subject. The Hanging Gardens were mine before any of the code was, and that is
what kept me pushing on it instead of settling for whatever came back first.

The rest of the breakthrough was the back-and-forth itself. Working through the
week with Claude, I kept noticing problems — sometimes in what it produced,
sometimes in what I had actually asked for — and each round of noticing made
the next version better. The improvement did not come from one good prompt. It
came from looking at what was on screen and being willing to say "that is not
it yet", over and over.

It was also my first time touching 3D. It is something I have been interested
in for a long time and never tried, and getting to build with it turned out to
be genuinely fun.

## What did this work change about who I want to be as a software developer?

I want to be a developer who can steer AI well. This week made that concrete
rather than abstract: the harness matters enormously for Claude Code, and so
does writing the plan before the building.

But I also learned the other half. When my own ideas change as much as they did
this week, the harness starts to cost something — every change of direction
means going back and rewriting `PLAN.md` and `CLAUDE.md` too. Keeping structure
without letting it become a tax on changing my mind is the balance I want to
get good at.
